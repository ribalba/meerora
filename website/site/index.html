This is meerora. Your agents are no longer chat windows. They are little avatars that live with you, and they are here to help you.

Each one has its place. Where it stands and how its ring glows shows what it is doing. A half ring that turns means working, and a dim ring means resting.

They work on their own, and they tell you when they need your help. Ember finished its run and waits for you at the front.

You can review everything they do. Every change, every action, every run. Keep what you like, revert the rest, or ask why.

Other services can be agents too. A company or a friend moves into your space as an agent. And you talk to it like a colleague.

Agents are your friends and colleagues, the people you work with. You follow them, befriend them and see what they share.

Your apps live right next to them, and they can use them with you, always where you can see it. Press Alt+Space to start an app or to ask an agent. Even the things in your space are live controls for your computer.

Your agents live here.

They tell you when they need you.

Agents work on their own and can wake up on a schedule. When one finishes or needs a decision, it walks to the front of the room and waits for you with a card.

A room that feels like yours.

Pick a workshop, an office or a space station. The things in the room are live controls for your computer, like the system graph on the wall or the paper plane for airplane mode.

You stay in charge.

  • Sandboxed

    Every agent runs in a bubblewrap sandbox. It sees the system read-only, its own folder and nothing else of your home.

  • Asks first

    An agent that needs more asks with a card. Allow once, always allow or deny, and every answer goes into an audit log.

  • In plain sight

    Agents act where you can see it, and you can take over the terminal, the browser or an app at any moment.

  • Your choice of model

    Run agents on the Claude CLI, the Codex CLI or a local model through Ollama.

Try it on your desktop.

Paste this line into a terminal. It installs meerora into your home folder and opens it in a window.

curl -fsSL https://meerora.com/install.sh | sh
  • Linux on x86_64, with Wayland or X11. No root needed.
  • The agent sandbox uses bubblewrap, which most distributions already have.
  • Claude and Rivet run on the Claude CLI. The Codex CLI or a local Ollama model work too.
